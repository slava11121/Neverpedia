Kivish Police Vehicle Fleet

The Kivish Police Department is the government service responsible for maintaining public order and patrolling cities, smaller settlements, and roads in the main territory of Kivish. The department divides its vehicle fleet into small, medium, capital-city, off-road, and high-speed patrols.
