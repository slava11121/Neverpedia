Gruzovoy Vozdulyot

Gruzovoy Vozdulyot is a Kivish cargo airline that operated international and domestic freight services. It is best known for Flight GV-467 (1985), which witnessed a major air disaster in Japan and sparked a high-profile conflict with air traffic control over demands to begin a rescue operation immediately.
